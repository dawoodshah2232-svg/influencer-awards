Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026. Short version: we collect only what the awards need, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

Nominees: legal name (private), display name, contact details, location, category, profile links, bio, photo and login credentials. Voters: a hashed mobile-number identifier and verification records — never shared with nominees. RSVP & enquiries: name, email and message content.

How we use it

To run the awards: review nominations, verify votes, prevent fraud, publish approved nominee profiles and results, manage the ceremony, and reply to messages. Marketing messages are sent only with your explicit consent.

What we never do

We never sell personal data. Voter identities are never disclosed to nominees or third parties. Legal names of nominees stay internal.

Retention & your rights

Records are kept as long as needed to run and audit the edition. You can request a copy, correction or deletion of your data — contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Production systems use encrypted storage, server-side sessions, multi-factor admin access and audit logging. Demo builds store data only in your own browser.
